Health Imp privacy policy

Effective November 5, 2026

In short: Health Imp keeps everything you enter on your own device. It does not collect, upload, share, or sell any of your information, and it has no accounts, no ads, and no tracking.

What Health Imp stores, and where

Health Imp is a personal notebook for your health. It stores what you enter in it: medications and when you took them, readings such as blood sugar, blood pressure, and weight, journal entries, appointments, lab results, symptoms, personal details such as your name, allergies, and emergency contact, insurance and doctor details, and your settings.

All of this is saved only on your device: in the app's private storage on a phone, or in your user account's app data folder on a computer. There is no Health Imp account and no Health Imp server.

What Health Imp collects

Nothing. Health Imp does not send your information anywhere. It contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking code, and it does not use advertising IDs. Its fonts and images are part of the app, so it does not download anything while you use it.

If you tap the feedback email address or the Ko-fi link, your email app or web browser opens. Anything you then send or visit is handled by that app or website, not by Health Imp.

Backups and copies you make

On Android, Health Imp is left out of Android's automatic Google Drive backup, so your records stay on your phone. To keep a copy, use Back up all data in the app, and keep the file somewhere you trust.

Health Imp only creates files when you ask it to: a backup, a CSV export of your log, a report for your doctor, or (on a computer) a daily Markdown copy of your log in its data folder. You choose where saved or shared files go, and once they leave the app, those copies are under your control.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show medication, appointment, and other reminders you set up.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms): so medication reminders arrive at the time you chose. You can turn this off; reminders may then arrive a few minutes late.
  • Run at startup and keep the device awake briefly: so reminders are rescheduled after your phone restarts and are delivered on time.
  • Network access: included by the app framework Health Imp is built on. Health Imp does not use it to send or receive any of your information.

Deleting your information

You can edit or delete any entry in the app, and remove a person and all their records in Settings. Uninstalling Health Imp deletes everything it stored on your device. Backup files you saved elsewhere are not affected; delete those yourself if you no longer want them.

Children

Health Imp is meant for adults managing their own health records. It does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Not medical advice

Health Imp is a notebook for your own records. It does not give medical advice, diagnose, or treat any condition. Talk with your doctor or pharmacist about your health and medications.
